About My Bows

For the last twenty-five years I have earned my living as a professional bassist on the first stand of three major Canadian orchestras. Always fascinated with the properties of wood and hair used in bows, I design and craft bows intended for demanding players.

My Philosophy: My bows are somewhat different than those commonly seen on the market. When making a bow, I concentrate on focus: a direct, clean, penetrating sound with controlled response.

Bow Specifications

All of my bows have a big arch and are very flexible in character. Designed to be pulled horizontally with minimal pressure, they require a smaller than usual tightening distance and are individually balanced.

I use only the finest quality Canadian white horse hair which I find offers the best grip. All bows are silver mounted and individually balanced for maximum results.

Bow Models

French Model Bows

  • Solo: Hair length ~55 cm, more bend and stiffer wood, 125-130 grams. Extremely responsive. Excellent for solo or fast passages.
  • General-Use: Hair length ~55 cm, less bend and looser hair, 125-135 grams. More air in the sound and bigger sound feel.
  • Sartory: 3/4 inch shorter with more distance between hair and stick, 125-130 grams. Kindlier and gentler response.
  • Massaranduba: Similar to Sartory and Vigneron models, 135-150 grams. Better consistency and vibration quality.

German Model Bows

Hair length ~55 cm. Weight between 115-130 grams.

Note: All 4 French models are good but it depends on the style you play. I prefer to first consult with my customer before beginning work on his/her order.
